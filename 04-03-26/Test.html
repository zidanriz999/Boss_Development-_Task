The Alchemical
Transition.

Founded in the quiet hours of a rainy autumn dusk, Playback Coffee began with a singular obsession: the resonance of flavor. We believe that roasting is not just a process, but a performance.

Our master roasters treat every batch like a master recording. We listen for the first crack, monitor the aromatic development, and pull the beans at the precise moment their terroir finds its highest expression.
